UR PIM App

A product information workspace for Urban Road. Artwork, products, variants and channel requirements, brought into one connected application.

From collection upload
to channel-ready data.

A growing product catalogue brings together artwork, sizes, finishes, pricing and channel-specific requirements. UR PIM gives that information a shared structure and a clear path from import to review and export.

The workspace connects collection uploads, product and variant records, validation findings and channel mappings. Staff can review the information in context before it moves into the next stage.

PIM AI access adds read-only connections for ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor and compatible MCP clients, using each person’s existing PIM permissions.

A connected catalogue workflow.

Capabilities shown in the application’s current public overview.

Collection imports

Turn a seed workbook into product, variant and content records. Preview the generated output and resolve findings before adding it to the catalogue.

Product and variant data

Keep artwork, sizes, finishes, content and pricing together, with saved views and activity history for day-to-day work.

Review in context

Bring the record, field and validation finding together so staff can resolve blockers and review warnings before saving.

Channel exports and APIs

Apply channel eligibility, mappings and overrides, preview export rows and connect other tools through the REST API.
