A few useful answers.

Can you work with our existing applications?

Yes. Discovery starts with the applications and data you already have. We assess API access, platform limits and your workflows before agreeing what to connect or build.

Do we need to replace all our systems?

Not necessarily. A focused application or integration may solve the problem. We can scope an initial release and build on it as your needs develop.

How are projects priced?

Pricing follows the agreed scope, complexity and delivery requirements. For work with significant unknowns, we start with a discovery engagement to define the requirements and prepare a phased proposal.

What happens after launch?

Handover includes agreed documentation and training. Maintenance, support coverage and future improvements can be scoped around the systems delivered and the level of help your team needs.

Can you work with an international team?

Yes. Projects can be delivered remotely. Communication times, milestones, review sessions and support coverage are agreed at the start.
