Privacy policy.

What we collect, why we use it and how to stay in control of your information.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

JUVERTECH IT SOLUTIONS is responsible for the personal information we handle through this website and our direct business inquiries. This policy explains those activities. Information handled within a client’s application or project is also subject to the relevant service agreement and that client’s privacy notice.

Information you choose to share

When you submit the contact form, your name, work email, company, selected service, timeline and project details are sent through EmailJS to JUVERTECH’s configured email inbox. We use your email address to reply to your inquiry. The form also lets you copy your brief to your clipboard or download it as a text file on your device.

If you call, text or message us on Messenger, we receive your contact details and the information you choose to include. Please share only what is needed to explain your request.

How we use your information

We use information you share to respond to inquiries, understand your requirements, prepare proposals and communicate about work you request. Where an inquiry becomes a project, we also use the relevant information to deliver the agreed services and meet applicable record-keeping obligations.

Depending on the activity, processing is based on steps you request before entering a contract, performance of an agreement, a legal obligation or your consent. Technical information needed to operate and protect the website is processed for those legitimate purposes. We do not use this website to make automated decisions about you.

Website access and browser storage

When you visit, your browser sends technical information to the hosting service, such as your IP address, browser details and the pages or files requested. This supports delivery, security and troubleshooting of the website.

This website does not set advertising cookies or load third-party analytics. Fonts and site assets are served locally. Browser local storage keeps demonstration-counter values between visits; it does not hold your contact-form entries. Form entries remain in the page during your visit and are sent only when you submit the form. You can remove this stored display data through your browser’s site-data settings. Your browser may separately remember form fields through its own autofill settings.

Service providers and external links

Hosting and communication providers process information as needed to deliver their services. EmailJS processes contact-form submissions and our connected email provider delivers and stores those inquiries. The Messenger widget loads no Meta scripts or tracking pixels; choosing its link opens Messenger, where Meta’s privacy policy applies. We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to establish or protect legal rights. We do not sell your personal information.

Links to other websites take you to services with their own privacy policies. Information you submit to those services is handled under their policies.

How long information is kept

Submitted inquiries are delivered to our email inbox and may also be recorded in EmailJS delivery history. Unsubmitted form entries are not stored by our website. Clipboard copies and downloaded files remain under your control. Local display data stays in your browser until you or the browser remove it.

For inquiries you submit, we retain information for as long as it is needed to address your request. If we work together, relevant records are kept for the duration of the engagement and any period needed for legal, accounting or dispute-resolution obligations. When those purposes no longer apply, the information is deleted or anonymized.

Sharing information safely

Please do not include passwords, API keys, payment-card details, customer records or other sensitive information in an initial inquiry. If a project requires access to confidential data or systems, we will agree an appropriate way to share it before that access is provided.

Your privacy rights

Subject to the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and applicable exceptions, you may ask to access or correct your personal information, object to processing, or request erasure or blocking. Where applicable, you may request a portable copy or withdraw consent for processing that relies on consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before the withdrawal.

To ask a privacy question or exercise your rights, call or text JUVERTECH IT SOLUTIONS at 0956 411 8702. Describe your request and how we can reply. We may need to verify your identity before providing or changing personal information.

You may also raise a concern with the National Privacy Commission.

Changes to this policy

We update this page when our information-handling practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Where a change requires notice or consent under applicable law, we will provide that notice or seek consent as required.
